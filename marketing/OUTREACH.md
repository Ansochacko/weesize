## Writers of “best free PDF tools” and privacy roundups

Subject: A local PDF toolkit, if you are updating that list

Hello,

I maintain a browser PDF toolkit. Files are not uploaded. The check I would ask a reviewer to run is: load the page, turn off Wi-Fi, and compress a file.

It does not do every job in a typical roundup. Encryption, true redaction, and OCR are labeled as missing rather than faked. If a local, no-account option is useful in the article, the privacy page is the one to cite.

Thank you for the piece either way.

## Universities and libraries

Subject: A no-upload PDF page for students

Hello,

Students are often told to shrink a PDF for a form, and the usual advice sends the file to a website. We published a browser tool that does that shrink on the device, with no account.

I am not asking you to endorse a file-size rule. If a page of yours already tells students to compress a PDF, a local option may belong next to that advice. I am happy to send a one-sentence description and then leave it.

One follow-up only if I do not hear back.
