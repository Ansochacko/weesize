Questions

Are my files sent to any server? ▾
No. Weesize processes documents entirely inside your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. Your files never leave your device.
Is Weesize really completely free? ▾
Yes. Weesize is free, open source, and has zero limits, subscriptions, watermarks, or accounts required.
How does exact-size compression work? ▾
Our client-side engine iteratively optimizes image streams and metadata targets until the file reaches your requested KB size without degrading legibility.
Does it work offline? ▾
Yes. Once the page is loaded, you can turn off Wi-Fi or disconnect from the internet and continue compressing and editing files.
